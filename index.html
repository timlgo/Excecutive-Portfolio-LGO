The method behind the pieces

How I work

It is not a branded methodology: it is the discipline of not giving an opinion before measuring.

1 · Raw data before the report

The hourly curve, the distribution by priority and the bursts only show up in record-level detail.

2 · Declare the fragile assumption

A model that does not declare where it can break is not a model, it is an opinion with a table.

3 · The artifact has to be usable

A template, a matrix, a shift proposal or a business case with CAPEX and OPEX that someone can apply on Monday.

4 · Adoption is part of the design

If the model demands data entry nobody will do, it is badly designed even if it is correct. In AI this applies twice.

5 · Say what did not work

Every piece includes what I would do differently: it is the section that shows independent judgment.
